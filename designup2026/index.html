01一Approach

What makes us tick?

Because we design beyond the brief. We build the experience from foundation to launch and beyond.

We align on the vision, understand the people and challenges we’re designing for, and build a strong foundation for what comes next. From a clear North Star to focused sprints, testing and launch, every step is designed to keep teams moving without compromising quality. AI, accessibility and design systems are built into the process from day one. And once the experience goes live, we stay with it, supporting, refining and improving it as it grows.

02二Capabilities

Our capabilities

Great design rarely lives in one discipline. It moves between strategy, product, people, technology, and craft.

Our capabilities come together across that entire journey, helping turn complex challenges into experiences that feel considered, useful and distinctly human.

  • Experience Design

    Every interaction tells part of the story. We bring user experience, research, journeys, information architecture, interactions and interfaces together to create experiences that feel connected, clear and intuitive from start to finish.

  • Experience Strategy

    Before designing the answer, we make sure we’re solving the right problem. Through research, synthesis, opportunity framing, market analysis and vision-setting, we uncover where experience can create meaningful value.

  • Content Strategy

    Good content knows what to say, where to say it, and how to sound. We bring together content strategy, voice and tone, taxonomy, localization and UX writing to give experiences a clear and consistent voice.

  • Service Design and Product Design

    Identify and launch new services, products, and business models with our rapid innovation, design thinking, and prototyping.

  • Visual Design

    From interfaces and style guides to illustrations and brand alignment, we create visual systems that give experiences clarity, character and consistency.

  • Physical and Emerging Experiences

    Use emerging experiences to deliver real value and foster innovation, testing, and learning to reach users anywhere.

03三AI

AI in the design loop

AI is becoming a core part of how dentsu works, builds and creates. We’re on a journey towards an AI-native way of working, embedding AI across client delivery, business functions, products and everyday workflows.

Our capabilities span:

  • Conversational AI
  • Voice commerce
  • Agentic customer solutions
  • Rapid prototyping
  • Synthetic personas
  • Insight generation
  • AI-powered content and productivity
  • AI evaluation frameworks

Within design, we’re embedding AI across the process, from competitor research and UX audits to Figma design, iteration, and Figma-to-code workflows. Plugins also support design systems, accessibility and quality checks, freeing designers to focus on creativity, problem-solving and the overall experience.
